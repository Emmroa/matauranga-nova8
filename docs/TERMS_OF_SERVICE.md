## 2. Not a medical or clinical service

**NOVA is not a doctor, nurse, therapist, or registered health professional.**

NOVA provides general health education and information. It does not provide:
- Medical diagnosis

- Prescriptions or treatment plans

**Nothing NOVA says should replace advice from a qualified health professional.** If you have a health concern, please contact your GP, a sexual health clinic, or call Healthline 0800 611 116.

If you are in immediate danger, call **111**.

## 3. Crisis protocol notice

NOVA automatically detects signs of distress, suicidal ideation, or acute crisis. If these signals are detected, NOVA will:

1. Acknowledge you warmly
2. Provide immediate crisis resources:
   - **111** — emergency services
   - **Lifeline 0800 543 354** — 24/7, free
   - **1737** — free text or call, 24/7

This is an automated safety feature. It is not a substitute for human crisis support. If you are in crisis, please contact one of the above services.

NOVA is powered by Mistral 7B, a large language model. AI models can:
- Make factual errors
- Misunderstand context

- Fail to detect nuance or urgency

Always verify health information with a qualified professional. NOVA's outputs are educational starting points, not final answers.
