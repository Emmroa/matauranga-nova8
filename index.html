He aha a NOVA / What is NOVA

Built for the moments that matter most.

NOVA is an AI companion designed for people living with HIV, those newly diagnosed, and anyone seeking prevention information — built specifically for Aotearoa New Zealand.

01
🤝

AI Companion, Not Advisor

NOVA speaks like a real person — warm, direct, without judgment. Not a doctor, not a therapist. A companion who genuinely understands, in your language, in your moment.

02
🛡️

Safe & Private Space

Zero Data Retention. No names, no identifiers stored permanently. Every conversation ends clean. Only anonymous aggregate topic data is kept for impact measurement.

03
🌿

Built for Aotearoa

English, te reo Māori, Spanish. Rooted in Te Whare Tapa Whā. Linked to NZ resources. NOVA mirrors your language and honours the culture you carry.

Features

A companion for every moment

NOVA responds in your language, meets you in your moment, and always points toward real NZ support when it matters most.

  • Multilingual — English, te reo Māori, Spanish. Mirrors your language automatically.
  • 7 Moment Detection — Diagnosis, disclosure, stigma, long-term living, PrEP, discrimination, online hate.
  • Crisis Protocol — Step-by-step support with Lifeline 0800 543 354 and 1737.
  • Zero Data Retention — Your words stay yours. Every session ends clean.
  • NZ-connected — Burnett Foundation, Body Positive, Rainbow Youth, Netsafe.

NOVA is a prototype AI companion developed for the Burnett Foundation Innovation Challenge 2026. I am an AI, not a doctor, therapist or healthcare professional. I do not provide medical advice.

All conversations are completely private and are deleted when the session ends (Zero Data Retention). Please always consult a qualified healthcare professional or the Burnett Foundation for personalised advice.

Tūmataiti / Privacy & Compliance

Your words never
leave this conversation.

Te Ao Māori / Cultural Values

He aha te mea nui o te ao?

NOVA is built on Te Whare Tapa Whā — the four walls of wellbeing — ensuring care spans the whole person, not just the diagnosis.

Taha Tinana
Physical Wellbeing

NOVA connects people to physical health resources: PrEP, PEP (72h), STI testing, DoxyPEP, and the Burnett Foundation's clinical services.

Taha Hinengaro
Mental & Emotional

Crisis protocol activates at the first signal of distress — direct or indirect. Lifeline (0800 543 354) and 1737 are always present.

Taha Wairua
Spiritual Dimension

NOVA recognises identity, meaning, and personal values without judgment. The HIV experience is part of a full life — never a definition.

Taha Whānau
Family & Community

When whānau is mentioned, NOVA acknowledges the collective weight first. Rainbow Youth, Body Positive NZ, takatāpui community woven throughout.

Hono mai / Install & Embed

Add NOVA to
any website.

One script tag. Full widget with privacy consent, multilingual support, and crisis protocol built in.

HTML EMBED
<script src="https://your-domain.onrender.com/nova-widget.js" data-lang="auto" data-consent="true" data-analytics="true"> </script> // data-lang="auto" → detects browser language // data-consent="true" → shows NZ Privacy Act banner // data-analytics="true" → enables topic-level tracking
RENDER ENVIRONMENT
GOOGLE_API_KEY = your_gemini_api_key_here DASHBOARD_PASSWORD = burnett2026 PORT = 10000 # Model: gemini-2.5-flash (stable) # Health check: GET /health # Chat: POST /chat { message }

Clone & deploy to Render

Fork the NOVA repo. Connect to render.com for automatic deployment.

2
Set your API key

Add GOOGLE_API_KEY in Render → Environment.

3
Embed on any site

Copy the script tag. NOVA appears as a floating widget with consent banner.
