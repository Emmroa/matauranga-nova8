He aha a NOVA / What is NOVA

Built for the moments
that matter most.

NOVA is an AI companion designed for people living with HIV, those newly diagnosed, and anyone seeking prevention information — built specifically for Aotearoa New Zealand.

01
🤝

AI Companion, Not Advisor

NOVA speaks like a real person — warm, direct, without judgment. Not a doctor, not a therapist. A companion who genuinely understands, in your language, in your moment.

02
🛡️

Safe & Private Space

Zero Data Retention. No names, no identifiers stored permanently. Every conversation ends clean. Only anonymous aggregate topic data is kept for impact measurement.

03
🌿

Built for Aotearoa

English, te reo Māori, Spanish. Rooted in Te Whare Tapa Whā. Linked to NZ resources. NOVA mirrors your language and honours the culture you carry.

Features

A companion for every moment

NOVA responds in your language, meets you in your moment, and always points toward real NZ support when it matters most.

  • Multilingual — English, te reo Māori, Spanish. Mirrors your language automatically — no need to ask.
  • 7 Moment Detection — New diagnosis, disclosure, stigma, long-term living, PrEP, discrimination, online hate.
  • Crisis Protocol — Step-by-step support with Lifeline 0800 543 354 and 1737 integration.
  • Zero Data Retention — Your words stay yours. The chat ends clean every time.
  • NZ-connected — Burnett Foundation, Body Positive, Rainbow Youth, Netsafe, emergency services built in.

Te Ao Māori / Cultural Values

He aha te mea nui o te ao?

NOVA is built on Te Whare Tapa Whā — the four walls of wellbeing — ensuring care spans the whole person, not just the diagnosis.

Taha Tinana
Physical Wellbeing

NOVA connects people to physical health resources: PrEP, PEP (72h), STI testing, DoxyPEP, and the Burnett Foundation's clinical services. Medical questions are always redirected to qualified NZ health professionals — never answered directly.

Taha Hinengaro
Mental & Emotional

Crisis protocol activates at the first signal of distress — direct or indirect. NOVA validates feelings before offering any information. Lifeline (0800 543 354) and 1737 are always present and referenced when needed.

Taha Wairua
Spiritual Dimension

NOVA recognises identity, meaning, and personal values without judgment. The HIV experience is acknowledged as part of a full life — never as a definition of who someone is.

Taha Whānau
Family & Community

When whānau is mentioned, NOVA acknowledges the collective weight before responding individually. Rainbow Youth, Body Positive NZ, and takatāpui community support are woven throughout every relevant moment.

Hono mai / Install & Embed

Add NOVA to
any website.

One script tag. Full widget with privacy consent, multilingual support, and crisis protocol built in.

HTML EMBED
<script src="https://your-domain.onrender.com/nova-widget.js" data-lang="auto" data-consent="true" data-analytics="true"> </script> // data-lang="auto" → detects browser language // data-consent="true" → shows NZ Privacy Act banner // data-analytics="true" → enables topic-level tracking
RENDER ENVIRONMENT
GOOGLE_API_KEY = your_gemini_api_key_here PORT = 10000 # Model: gemini-2.5-flash (stable, free tier) # Health check: GET /health # Chat endpoint: POST /chat { message, sessionId }
1
Clone & deploy to Render

Fork the NOVA GitHub repo. Connect to render.com for automatic deployment on every push.

2
Set your Gemini API key

Add GOOGLE_API_KEY in Render → Environment. Get a free key at aistudio.google.com. Use gemini-2.5-flash.

3
Embed on your site

Copy the script tag into any webpage. NOVA appears as a floating widget with consent banner — works on burnettnz.co.nz or any site.

4
Monitor via Dashboard

Visit dashboard.html to see anonymised impact analytics — topics, crisis activations, languages, geographic spread.
