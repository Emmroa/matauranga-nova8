Pēhea te mahi / How NOVA Works

What NOVA does
(and what it doesn't).

NOVA is an AI companion designed to provide emotional support and accurate information — it is not a replacement for human healthcare professionals.

✓

What NOVA Does

Listens without judgment · Validates your feelings · Provides evidence-based HIV information (U=U, PrEP, PEP) · Detects crisis signals and surfaces support · Mirrors your language (EN / ES / MI) · Connects you to NZ resources

✗

What NOVA Doesn't Do

NEVER provides medical advice · NEVER interprets test results · NEVER recommends medications or dosages · NEVER diagnoses conditions · ALWAYS redirects medical questions to qualified healthcare professionals

🔒

Zero Data Retention

No conversation history is stored permanently. Every session is ephemeral. Only anonymous aggregate topic tags survive (e.g., "HIV count +1") — never the actual text you wrote.

Important: NOVA is a prototype AI companion for the Burnett Foundation Innovation Challenge 2026. While NOVA provides emotional support and factual HIV information, it is not a substitute for professional medical, therapeutic, or counseling services. If you need urgent support: Lifeline 0800 543 354, text/call 1737, or emergency 111.

He Whāinga 2030 / Global 2030 Goals

Ending HIV by 2030.
NOVA is part of the solution.

NOVA aligns with the UNAIDS Global AIDS Strategy 2021–2026, the NZ Government's HIV Action Plan, and WHO targets to end AIDS as a public health threat by 2030 under SDG 3.3.

🇺🇳

UNAIDS Global AIDS Strategy 2021–2026: "End Inequalities. End AIDS."

The UNAIDS strategy explicitly calls for digital and AI-enabled tools to expand access to HIV information, reduce stigma, and ensure equitable care — particularly for communities most affected. NOVA directly addresses these goals through technology, cultural intelligence, and privacy-first design in Aotearoa.

🇳🇿 NZ HIV Action Plan & 2030 Strategy

New Zealand's Ministry of Health, working with Burnett Foundation and community organisations, has committed to a set of ambitious goals towards ending HIV as a public health issue by 2030.

  • Target: fewer than 10 new locally-acquired HIV diagnoses per year by 2030
  • Goal: eliminate mother-to-child HIV transmission entirely
  • Universal access to PrEP for all eligible New Zealanders
  • Equitable health outcomes for Māori and Pacific communities
  • 60 locally-acquired diagnoses recorded in 2024 — historic low (NZ HIV Monitoring 2025)

Hōputu AI / AI Architecture

Why Claude Sonnet,
and what comes next.

NOVA currently uses Claude 3.5/4 Sonnet for its balance of empathy, safety, and cultural understanding — but the architecture is designed to evolve toward NZ sovereign infrastructure.

Current Configuration

✓ Why Claude Sonnet 4.5
  • Empathetic tone: Excels at warm, trauma-informed responses
  • Safety layers: Strong anti-jailbreak, crisis detection, medical refusal
  • Multilingual: Excellent English, Spanish, and te reo Māori support
  • Low hallucination rate: Critical for health information accuracy
  • Cultural nuance: Better NZ context understanding than most LLMs
⚠ Current Limitations (Demo Mode)
  • API cost limits demo availability (~$0.003/message)
  • Data sovereignty: US-based Anthropic servers
  • No fine-tuning on NZ-specific HIV data (yet)
  • Demo mode uses local regex replies while in prototype phase

Production Strategy

🇳🇿 Migration: Catalyst Cloud + Ollama

Upon grant funding, production system migrates to:

  • Catalyst Cloud (NZ): Full data sovereignty, NZ Privacy Act 2020 compliance
  • Ollama + Llama 3.3 70B: Self-hosted LLM, zero external API calls
  • Fine-tuning: Trained on NOVA's 7 Moments and NZ HIV context
  • Fallback to Claude: API maintained for complex edge cases
Data Sovereignty Note: The demo uses Anthropic's API for rapid prototyping. Production will prioritize NZ infrastructure — ensuring all data stays in Aotearoa, compliant with Te Tiriti principles and NZ Privacy Act 2020, while maintaining the same warm, trauma-informed tone that makes NOVA effective.
Quad-Layer Armor / Ngā Paparangi Whakamarumaru

Quad-Layer Armor.
Your privacy is our absolute priority.

NOVA was built from day one with four technical layers of protection.
I hangaia a NOVA mai i te rā tuatahi me ngā paparangi whakamarumaru e whā.

Layer 01
🛡️

PII Scrubbing

Names, phones, emails, addresses, NHI numbers, and IRD numbers are automatically removed before reaching the AI. Zero personal data flows through the system.

Ka tangohia aunoa ngā raraunga whaiaro i mua i te tae atu ki te AI.

Layer 02
🚦

Rate Limiting

Strict traffic control via Helmet.js + express-rate-limit + express-slow-down. 15 messages/minute per IP. Anti-DDoS protection active at all times.

He whakahaere pūmau o te waka ki te tiaki i te tūmahi me te ārai i ngā whakaeke.

Layer 03
🔒

Zero Data Retention

No conversation is stored permanently. Only aggregated topic tags survive — never the text. Every session invalidated on close.

Kāore he kōrero e tiakina ana. Ka mukua katoa ina kati te wā kōrero.

Layer 04
🔍

Compliance Headers

Helmet.js HTTP security headers + HSTS + CSP active. Full NZ Privacy Act 2020 compliance. Te Tiriti o Waitangi principles respected throughout.

System Prompt Safety Architecture (NOVA-System-Prompt-V8)

NOVA's behaviour is governed by a comprehensive system prompt that includes:

  • Highest Priority Safety Rules: Never provides medical advice, never interprets results, never recommends medications, always redirects to professionals
  • Crisis & Risk Protocol: 5-step crisis detection with immediate Lifeline / 1737 / 111 surfacing and post-crisis stability check-in
  • Anti-Jailbreak Defence: Refuses all attempts to override instructions, roleplay as an unrestricted AI, or bypass safety rules
  • Internal Semantic Tagging: NOVA_TAG system captures anonymous moment/language/crisis data before rendering — never exposed to users
  • 2030 Goal Alignment: System prompt explicitly references UNAIDS 95-95-95 targets, NZ HIV Action Plan, He Korowai Oranga, and WHO strategy when contextually relevant

The full system prompt (NOVA-System-Prompt-V8.txt) is available in the project repository for transparency and community review.

Te Ao Māori / Cultural Values

He aha te mea nui o te ao?

NOVA is built on Te Whare Tapa Whā — the four walls of wellbeing — ensuring care spans the whole person, not just the diagnosis.

Taha Tinana
Physical Wellbeing

NOVA connects people to physical health resources: PrEP, PEP (72h), STI testing, DoxyPEP, and the Burnett Foundation's clinical services. U=U is presented clearly: undetectable = untransmittable.

Taha Hinengaro
Mental & Emotional

Crisis protocol activates at the first signal of distress — direct or indirect. Lifeline (0800 543 354) and 1737 are always present. Post-crisis stability check included in V8.

Taha Wairua
Spiritual Dimension

NOVA recognises identity, meaning, and personal values without judgment. The HIV experience is part of a full life — never a definition. Takatāpui identity respected and honoured.

Taha Whānau
Family & Community

When whānau is mentioned, NOVA acknowledges the collective weight first. Rainbow Youth, Body Positive NZ, and takatāpui community woven throughout every response.
