Goal: <10 new local diagnoses/year · NZ HIV Action Plan

New Zealand has made significant progress toward ending HIV as a public health issue. The 2024 HIV Annual Surveillance Report (ESR / Ministry of Health) recorded 60 locally acquired diagnoses — the lowest since comprehensive recording began — reflecting the impact of expanded PrEP access, community testing programmes, and reduced stigma.

Despite progress, disparities persist. Gay, bisexual, and men who have sex with men (MSM) continue to represent the majority of new diagnoses. Māori and Pacific peoples face disproportionate barriers to testing and treatment access. Stigma remains a primary driver of late diagnosis.

NOVA's role: NOVA directly addresses the stigma and access barriers identified in the 2024 report — providing 24/7 confidential information, normalising testing, and connecting people to NZ services in their own language.

Sources: ESR HIV Annual Surveillance Report 2024 · UNAIDS 2024 Global AIDS Update · NZ Ministry of Health HIV Action Plan · Burnett Foundation Aotearoa
